# Quality and learning

## Decision and review records

Every substantial task records its source, current version, dependencies, actual worker, output, review evidence and next action. A review states what was inspected and distinguishes pass, fail and not tested. A human’s acceptance of a limitation does not erase it. Release authorization records the exact human instruction, scope, visibility, destination and candidate revision; a later change requires checking whether that authorization still covers the candidate.

Automated checks verify structure, hashes, relationships and recorded states. Human judgment assesses author identity, cultural accuracy, literary voice, suitability for children and creative effect. Never call an AI review native-speaker, specialist or human editorial review. A different persona pass by the same assistant remains self-review.

## Corrections become scoped knowledge

Use the correction template in templates.json. Save the original, accepted revision, explanation, source/asset version and actual approval evidence. Specify whether the lesson applies to one passage, author, publication, language pair or general practice. Record superseding corrections; preserve earlier decisions as history. Do not automatically turn one preference into a universal rule or put unaccepted generated text into the approved-example library.

Examples from the project: disconnected or missing elephant limbs; illustrations contradicting a story; broad cover masks obscuring artwork; too much 3D tilt; unreadable author spacing; central disclosure; source-confirmed translations and author-name ambiguity. Source receipts remain in `provenance/`; reference them instead of replacing them or retroactively claiming human approval.

## Evaluate change

Compare the current workflow with a candidate on the same held-out, authorized works. Track source omissions/additions, author mismatches, literary voice and emotional effect, cultural errors, image anatomy and scene fidelity, cover legibility, linked-content completeness, correction rounds, time and cost when measured. Use separate scores or findings; a good average must not hide a wrong author or a missing stanza.

Ask human editors to compare candidates without model labels where practical. Record reviewer language capability and scope. Do not use the generator’s own preference as the sole quality measure. Test old failure cases after changing prompts, model providers, skills or rendering. Preserve the baseline and retain changes only when evidence supports improvement.

## Improvement stages

1. Version rules, prompts and publication profiles; retrieve relevant approved examples and cultural references for each task.
2. Collect contextual corrections and maintain a held-out evaluation set.
3. Compare capable text, vision and image tools on actual tasks; retain provider/model/version in records when known. Do not fabricate unavailable identifiers.
4. Consider separate text or image customization only for measured persistent weaknesses and material authorized for that use. Publication rights do not automatically confer training rights. Private manuscripts and children’s information are not a default training corpus.

This model’s memory is explicitly stored and retrieved. No model weights change through ordinary use. No training, billing, accounts or autonomous schedule is created by adoption.
