ଡିଜିଟାଲ୍ ରୂପସଜ୍ଜା ଓ ଚିତ୍ର ପରିକଳ୍ପନା

AI agents worked under the direction of Ahimanikya Satapathy, Creative Editor.

Creative Editor: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Illustration direction and digital design.

ୱେବ୍ ପତ୍ରିକାର ରୂପସଜ୍ଜା, ମଲାଟ ଓ ନୂତନ ଚିତ୍ରର ପରିକଳ୍ପନାର ଶ୍ରେୟ ଅହିମାଣିକ୍ୟ ଶତପଥୀଙ୍କୁ।

AI ସହଯୋଗୀ ଦଳ

ସୃଜନଶୀଳ ସମ୍ପାଦକ ଅହିମାଣିକ୍ୟ ଶତପଥୀଙ୍କ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶନାରେ AI ଏଜେଣ୍ଟମାନେ କାମ କରିଛନ୍ତି।

Our AI team structure follows the Project Odisha Blueprint. These names identify AI working roles, not human staff.

  • Disha Dash — Planning and coordination.
  • Samanta Chandrasekhar — Experience design and engineering.
  • Anvesha Acharya — Research, sources and archive stewardship.
  • Drishti Senapati — Quality and usability review.

The magazine’s human editors, including the Creative Editor, are peers at the same organizational level. All four AI agents report directly to Ahimanikya Satapathy, Creative Editor. Disha coordinates their work. Original writing and artwork remain credited to their creators.

ଚିତ୍ର ବିଷୟରେ

ପତ୍ରିକାର ନୂତନ ଡିଜିଟାଲ୍ ମଲାଟ ଓ ଅନେକ ଗଳ୍ପଚିତ୍ର ତିଆରିରେ କୃତ୍ରିମ ବୁଦ୍ଧିମତ୍ତା (AI) ଉପକରଣ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରାଯାଇଛି। ଚିତ୍ରର ବିଷୟ, ଶୈଳୀ ଓ ସଂଶୋଧନ ମଣିଷଙ୍କ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶନାରେ ହୁଏ।

New digital cover artwork and many story illustrations are AI-generated, with human creative direction, selection and revision. This disclosure applies across the magazine, including its archive and illustrated digital books.

ମୂଳ ଲେଖା ଓ କଳାକୃତି

ଗଳ୍ପ, କବିତା ଓ ଅନ୍ୟ ଲେଖାର ଶ୍ରେୟ ସମ୍ପୃକ୍ତ ଲେଖକଙ୍କର। ପିଲାଙ୍କ ଚିତ୍ର, ମୂଳ ମୁଦ୍ରିତ ମଲାଟ ଓ ସ୍କାନ୍ କରାଯାଇଥିବା ବହିର ଚିତ୍ର ସେମାନଙ୍କ ମୂଳ ସ୍ରଷ୍ଟାଙ୍କ କୃତି। ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ AI-ନିର୍ମିତ ବୋଲି ଦର୍ଶାଯାଉନାହିଁ।

Original writing remains credited to its authors. Children’s drawings, original printed artwork and scanned books retain their original credits and are not covered by the AI-generated artwork description above. Existing book-specific illustration credits remain in place.
