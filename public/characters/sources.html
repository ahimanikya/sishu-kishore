Abolakara & Dharmapada: the traditional references

The two new casts borrow broad storytelling or craft themes; their identities, relationships, contemporary settings and pilot scripts are original project development.

Stories Along the Way
Abolakara supplies the broad relationship between a questioning companion and a storyteller. These names, family relationship, contemporary setting, appearances and scripts are new project work. No particular Manoj Das or Mohanty story is being relabelled.
The Young Makers
Dharmapada is a traditional legendary reference for youthful craft and insight. Tara, Dinu and Kanu have their own contemporary lives and original plots. They do not depict Dharmapada or Bisu Maharana, and this series does not rewrite the legend’s ending.
  • Kailash Pattanaik, “Abolkara: The Odyssey of a Disobedient Hero”, Lokaratna V & VI (2013), pp. 143–145 — the questioning travelling companion and later literary versions.
  • Black Eagle Books: Abolakara Kahani, Abhay Charan Mohanty — the collection and master–companion story frame.
  • Odisha Review, December 2015, Konark account — Dharmapada as a legendary young craftsman; a legend is not a verified biography.
  • Odisha Review, September–October 2021 — Dharama/Dharmapada naming and the legendary tradition.
