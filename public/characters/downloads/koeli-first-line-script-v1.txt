Concept and creative direction: Ahimanikya Satapathy
ଆରମ୍ଭଟା କେଉଁଠି? / Where Did the Beginning Go?
First film-script draft. Timing is a target, not a recorded performance.
Independent fluent Odia review pending.

SCENE 1 — 0–8 sec
ସକାଳ। ଡାଳରେ କୋଇଲି ଆଗକୁ ଢଳେ। ତଳେ ବସିଥିବା ପିଲାମାନେ ଚିତ୍ରର ବାହାରେ।
Morning. Koeli leans forward on a branch. The listening children are below, outside the frame.
Sound: ପତ୍ରର ହାଲୁକା ଶବ୍ଦ; ଏକ ଛୋଟ କୁହୁ ଡାକ। / Soft leaves; one short kuhu call.
କୋଇଲି: ଶେଷରେ ବ୍ୟାଗ୍‌ଟା ଗଛରେ ମିଳିଲା!
Koeli: And in the end, they found the bag in the tree!

SCENE 2 — 8–20 sec
କୋଇଲି ଉତ୍ତର ପାଇଁ ଅପେକ୍ଷା କରେ। ତଳୁ ଜଣେ ପିଲା ପଚାରେ।
Koeli waits for a reaction. A child calls up from below.
Sound: ଏକ ଛୋଟ ନିରବ ବିରାମ। / A brief quiet pause.
ପିଲା: କାହା ବ୍ୟାଗ୍?
Child: Whose bag?
କୋଇଲି: ଓହୋ! ଆରମ୍ଭଟା ତ କହିନି!
Koeli: Oh! I forgot the beginning!

SCENE 3 — 20–32 sec
କୋଇଲି ମୁଣ୍ଡ ଢଳାଇ ଭାବେ। ପିଲାଟି ହସେ; କୋଇଲି ତା’ ସହ ହସେ।
Koeli tilts its head to think. The child laughs, and Koeli joins in.
Sound: ଛୋଟ ହସ; ପତ୍ର ଶବ୍ଦ ଚାଲିଥାଏ। / A small laugh over the continuing leaves.
ପିଲା: ଏଥର ଆଗରୁ କହ।
Child: Start at the beginning this time.
କୋଇଲି: ହଁ। ପ୍ରଥମେ ଗୋଟେ ବ୍ୟାଗ୍ ଥିଲା…
Koeli: Right. First, there was a bag…

SCENE 4 — 32–45 sec
କୋଇଲି ପୁଣି ଅଟକେ। ତଳକୁ ଚାହେଁ। ଶୁଣିବାରେ ଗପ ଶେଷ।
Koeli stops again and looks down. End on the act of listening.
Sound: ହାଲୁକା ହସ, ତା’ପରେ ନିରବତା। / A gentle laugh, then quiet.
ପିଲା: ଆଉ ବ୍ୟାଗ୍‌ଟା କାହାର ଥିଲା?
Child: And whose bag was it?
କୋଇଲି: ଆଚ୍ଛା, ତୁମେ ପଚାର। ମୁଁ କହୁଛି।
Koeli: All right. You ask, and I’ll tell you.
