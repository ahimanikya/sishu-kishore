Recommended order of work

1 Finish language decisions. Use the prepared review copies for Nilu–Chaiti and Koraput. Apply specific feedback across scripts, lettering, exported pages and product labels, then check the affected layouts. Self-review is already recorded; independent review is still open.

2 Settle the identities. Confirm or replace working names. For Nilu–Chaiti, decide whether the animal companions need personal names and whether a fixed place or relationship adds value. For Koraput, confirm the proposed family and names before expanding the series.

3 Extend the strongest creative need. Nilu–Chaiti is the practical choice for a small paper-product pilot because the range is already broad. A second Koraput story would test whether its family dynamic works beyond the first outing. These are development recommendations, not evidence of market demand.

4 Resume printing when ready. Use the chosen printer’s specifications to prepare the selected files and a small sample batch. Check type, trim, colour, stock, binding and finish on the actual sample. Pricing and quantities can follow supplier quotes and sample results.

For sharing now, use this report to discuss characters and direction. The digital storybooks and product packs are supporting creative material; no physical stock, sales performance or calibrated production result is represented.

What each direction is best placed to explore

Gudul–Gudli · adaptation and character acting
Useful for discussing how an existing literary cast can become expressive visual storytelling. Keep its source history and unresolved questions visible.
Nilu–Chaiti · stories that become activities
The strongest existing starting point for a small paper-goods sample range, because the stories, activity book and multiple product layouts already exist. Commercial performance remains untested.
Koraput · a continuing regional family series
The most specific recurring home and relationship structure. A second story would help assess whether the family creates varied situations beyond the pilot’s path puzzle.
