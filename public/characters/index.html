04 / Questions, journeys and shared stories

Stories Along the Way

Benu always has another question. His storytelling uncle Madhab has a story for almost everything—and is learning to admit when he does not know. Their short journeys bring curiosity, humour and mutual discovery.

05 / Make, test, notice and try again

The Young Makers

Tara sees what a thing could become. Kanu tests what it actually does. Tara’s artisan uncle Dinu brings experience, patience and the occasional habit of helping too quickly. Their workshop adventures turn mistakes into shared discoveries.

Gudul–Gudli is the earlier source-linked literary adaptation study. Nilu–Chaiti is a newly developed cast with documented carryover of project-created personality themes and short-story premises. The Koraput cast is a separate fictional family world.

The original casts use working names. Completed items are digital creative deliverables; fluent Odia feedback and physical product samples are still pending. New story possibilities in this expanded edition are marked as proposals.
